ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Built to help protect
our marine ecosystems.

The COTS Monitoring System is a capstone project focused on supporting the identification, reporting, and monitoring of crown-of-thorns starfish through a mobile-first experience.

THE CHALLENGE

Understanding the problem.

Crown-of-thorns starfish (COTS) feed on coral. When their populations grow to outbreak levels, they can contribute to extensive coral loss. Consistent observations and reliable documentation can help support marine management efforts.

✳

Our project objective

Develop a practical digital platform that helps divers and authorized monitoring personnel record possible COTS detections, review evidence, and track follow-up activities.

SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

Designed around real workflows.

These are the intended system capabilities. Final availability depends on implementation and testing.

◎

Image-based Detection

AI-assisted identification of potential COTS in submitted underwater images.

⌖

GPS & Mapping

Associate observations with geographic locations for spatial monitoring.

▤

Detection History

View previously submitted records and their monitoring status.

✓

Expert Validation

Support review of submitted evidence before records are confirmed.

↗

Removal Documentation

Record follow-up evidence and track reported elimination activities.

▥

Monitoring Analytics

Provide a foundation for dashboards and summaries of recorded COTS activity.
